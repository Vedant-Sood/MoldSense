# Historical firmware

- `moldsense_firmware/`: BSEC/capture firmware with DHT22 reference support.
- `moldsensecodev2.0/`: earlier switch-controlled BSEC/capture variant.
- `bme688_logger/`: earlier logger and download/file-transfer utilities.

Original READMEs describe their workflows. They use different commands/protocols from the integrated research firmware. They were not rebuilt or flashed for this import. Device backups, calibration state and private experimental captures were excluded. Historical model exports remain local and are not activated as validated mold models.
